440MW CCGT IPP project under FIDIC Silver Book

Ireland

Commercial and technical advice on 440MW CCGT IPP - project value  EUR407 million.

Financed by European Investment Bank (EIB) Loan of  EUR200 million in direct project finance/long-term debt

The remaining  EUR200 million was financed directly by the developer`s corporate resources and commercial borrowings underwritten by a state backed by utility balance sheet.

Plant construction under FIDIC Silver Book began in March 2008 and was completed on budget and entered commercial operations in late 2010.

Plant consists of a GE Frame 9FB (PG9371FB) gas turbine on a single-shaft configuration with GE A15 steam turbine with a SSS clutch driving a GE 450H generator.

Steam raised in CMI Energy Heat Recovery Steam Generator

The plant features several Innovative Features

Supplementary Firing; in post-combustion duct burner system designed to fire natural gas or Refinery Off-Gas (ROG) supplied directly from an adjacent oil refinery

Industrial Cogeneration; facility to export up to 50 tonnes per hour of high-pressure process steam (at 45 barg) across fence to an adjacent oil refinery

GE 450H synchronous condenser:

Generator can be configured to operate as a synchronous condenser plant can accommodate modification for a separate SSS clutch to decouple the generator.

Reliability Excellence; a new advanced software solution which taps into industrial-scale data analytics to predictively identify operational issues before they occur.
